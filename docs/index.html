Cape Cove

Cozy seaside merge game for iPhone.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Cape Cove does not collect, store or share any personal data. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

  • Game progress (coins, items, buildings, settings) is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.
  • Game Center: if you are signed in to Game Center, your scores are sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; the developer does not receive or store this data.
  • Notifications: reminders (for example "Energy is full") are scheduled locally on your device only if you turn them on in the game's settings. No data leaves your device.
  • In-app purchases: this version has no real-money purchases.
  • Children: the game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

If this policy changes (for example, if ads or purchases are added), this page will be updated before the change is released.
