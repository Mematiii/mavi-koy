**Description**
Welcome to Cape Cove, a sunny little seaside town that needs your help!

Merge two matching items to create something new: coffee beans become hot coffee, seashells become dolphins, seedlings become whole gardens. Tap your producers to make items, complete orders for friendly townsfolk and earn coins to restore the town.

• 57 merge chains with hundreds of items to discover
• Restore 60 buildings across 6 areas: Beach, Town, Village, Funfair, Forest and Snowy Peak
• Fill orders from colorful characters and earn coins, XP and stars
• Collect cards in the Town Album and complete sets for big rewards
• Daily tasks, weekly goals, a Travel Pass and a weekly Town Contest
• Game Center leaderboards
• Relaxing, no timers to rush you, plays offline

Grab your toolbox and start merging. Cape Cove is waiting!

**App Review Information → Notes**
No account or login is required. All content is in the app and works offline.
How to play: tap the green PLAY button (or the board icon) to open the merge board, tap a producer (e.g. the coffee machine) to create items, drag two matching items together to merge them, and tap GIVE on an order when its items are on the board. Coins from orders restore buildings on the town map.
There are no real-money purchases in this version; gems are earned in-game.
iOS features used:
• Game Center: weekly contest score and player level are submitted to leaderboards (Town Contest → "Game Center" button).
• Haptics: when merging, producing items and completing orders (Profile → Vibration).
• Local notifications: optional "Energy is full" and daily task reminders (Profile → Reminders).
